# 04: Area-Weighted Bimetallic Galvanic Mixed-Potential Solver

**What to build:** A numerical bimetallic galvanic coupling solver that takes the polarization parameters (and optional uncertainty distributions) of two electrically coupled metals under the same **Solution** and temperature along with a configurable cathode-to-anode area ratio ($A_{\text{noble}} / A_{\text{active}}$, default $1.0$), identifies the **Active Metal** and **Noble Metal**, solves area-weighted total current conservation via Brent's root-finding method (`brentq`) to determine the steady-state **Mixed Potential ($E_{\text{galv}}$)**, evaluates the coupled **Galvanic Current Density ($i_{\text{galv}}$)**, **Acceleration Factor**, and accelerated **Initial Corrosion Rate ($\text{CR}_0$)** on the **Active Metal** (with Monte Carlo uncertainty bands when parameter uncertainties are present), renders overlaid bimetallic Tafel plots annotated with the $E_{\text{galv}}$ operating line, and demonstrates galvanic coupling analysis in a dedicated notebook.

**Blocked by:** 02: Phase 1 Polarization Curve, Faraday Corrosion Rate & Galvanic Driving Force Screening

**Status:** resolved

- [x] For any pair of metals with distinct corrosion potentials ($E_{\text{corr, active}} < E_{\text{corr, noble}}$), the solver finds a unique **Mixed Potential ($E_{\text{galv}}$)** strictly within $(E_{\text{corr, active}}, E_{\text{corr, noble}})$ satisfying area-weighted current conservation $i_{\text{net, active}}(E_{\text{galv}}) + (A_{\text{noble}} / A_{\text{active}}) i_{\text{net, noble}}(E_{\text{galv}}) = 0$ to within numerical tolerance ($< 10^{-6}\text{ }\mu\text{A/cm}^2$).
- [x] The solver computes **Galvanic Current Density ($i_{\text{galv}}$)** on the **Active Metal**, **Acceleration Factor** ($i_{\text{galv}} / i_{\text{corr, active}} \ge 1.0$), and accelerated Faraday **Initial Corrosion Rate ($\text{CR}_0$)**, with **Acceleration Factor** increasing monotonically as $A_{\text{noble}} / A_{\text{active}}$ increases.
- [x] When parameter uncertainties are supplied, Monte Carlo propagation produces mean, standard deviation, and percentile confidence bands for $E_{\text{galv}}$, $i_{\text{galv}}$, **Acceleration Factor**, and accelerated $\text{CR}_0$.
- [x] Overlaid bimetallic Tafel plots clearly display both metals' polarization branches, the solved **Mixed Potential ($E_{\text{galv}}$)** operating line, and the shift from isolated $i_{\text{corr, active}}$ to coupled $i_{\text{galv}}$.
- [x] Automated behavioral tests and the galvanic analysis demonstration notebook verify root-finding accuracy, area-ratio scaling, uncertainty propagation, and plot rendering.
