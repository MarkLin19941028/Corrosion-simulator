# Log-space corrosion current prediction and physics-clipped Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation

Machine-learning models predict $\log_{10}(i_{\text{corr}})$ rather than linear $i_{\text{corr}}$, and Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation samples $\log_{10}(i_{\text{corr}}) \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_{\log i}, \sigma_{\log i}^2)$ before exponentiating ($i_{\text{corr}} = 10^x$), while sampling $(E_{\text{corr}}, \beta_a, \beta_c)$ in linear space with hard clipping of $\beta_a, \beta_c$ to $[20, 500]\text{ mV/dec}$. Because corrosion current densities span several orders of magnitude ($10^{-2}$ to $10^{3}\text{ }\mu\text{A/cm}^2$), linear-space regression and additive Gaussian noise would yield unphysical negative currents and negative Corrosion Rates during uncertainty propagation.
