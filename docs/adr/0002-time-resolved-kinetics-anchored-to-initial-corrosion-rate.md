# Time-resolved corrosion kinetics anchored to initial Faraday corrosion rate

All three time-resolved corrosion depth models (Linear, Passivation, and Power-law) are parameterized directly by the instantaneous Faraday corrosion rate $\text{CR}_0$ at $t=0$ rather than fitting independent rate constants detached from the polarization curve. Specifically, the Passivation model sets its decay constant to $k = \text{CR}_0 / d_{\max}$ so that $\left.\frac{dd}{dt}\right|_{t=0} = \text{CR}_0$, and the Power-law model anchors its prefactor at a reference time $t_{\text{ref}}$ via $d(t) = (\text{CR}_0 \cdot t_{\text{ref}})(t / t_{\text{ref}})^n$. This guarantees physical consistency between the Tafel/Faraday calculation and the time-resolved depth curves even when time-series etching curves are absent from the electrochemical database.
